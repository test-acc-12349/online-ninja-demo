Best Hotels in Melbourne

Are you planning a trip to Melbourne and need the perfect place to rest your head after a day of exploring? Well, mate, you've come to the right place. We've rounded up the best hotels in Melbourne, from luxurious five-star accommodations to funky boutique stays.

Luxury Accommodations

1. Crown Towers Melbourne

Let's start with the crème de la crème - Crown Towers Melbourne. Overlooking the Yarra River, this place is luxury on steroids. It's got everything from a state-of-the-art gym to its own shopping precinct. Just imagine sinking into their plush beds after a day of sightseeing.

2. The Langham Melbourne

You know what they say, "Location, location, location," and The Langham Melbourne checks this box with gusto. Located on Southbank Promenade, it offers stunning city views and is a stone's throw away from the city's best restaurants and shopping spots.

Boutique Hotels

3. Ovolo Laneways

No, we haven't forgotten about you hipsters out there. Ovolo Laneways is the place for you. Located in the heart of Melbourne's laneway culture, this hotel embraces the city's quirky side with uniquely styled rooms and free minibar goodies.

4. Adelphi Hotel

Calling all sweet tooths! You'll love the dessert-themed rooms at Adelphi Hotel. This boutique hotel in Flinders Lane screams funky from its rooftop to its basement. Plus, the in-house dessert restaurant, Om Nom, will have you drooling over their delightful creations.

Affordable Comfort

5. Alto Hotel on Bourke

Being on a budget doesn't mean you have to compromise on comfort. Alto Hotel on Bourke, a carbon-neutral hotel, proves this. It's got comfy rooms, free Wi-Fi, and even a library - a real home away from home.

6. Ibis Melbourne Central

For those of you who know that ibis is not just a bird, the Ibis Melbourne Central is another great budget-friendly option. Clean rooms, central location, and all the essentials you need - no frills, just good vibes.

Hotel Categories Overview

  • Luxury: Crown Towers, The Langham
  • Boutique: Ovolo Laneways, Adelphi Hotel
  • Budget-Friendly: Alto Hotel, Ibis Melbourne Central
